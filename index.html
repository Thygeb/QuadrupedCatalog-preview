How to read the figures

33.8kg
The manufacturer states a figure with a unit.
0
The manufacturer states the value zero. That is a measured number, not an empty field.
yes
The manufacturer states that the robot has this.
no
The manufacturer states that the robot does not have this.
not stated
The manufacturer states nothing about this field.
visible in a photo only
The information can be seen in the manufacturer’s photo but is written nowhere.
14kgA
A superscript letter after a figure points to the source the figure came from. Robots with several sources carry several letters.
1,100mmB
A dashed letter marks a secondary source on the manufacturer’s own domain – a datasheet, manual or developer documentation, not the product page.
Payload, walking
A stroke before a field name means the figure carries a caveat that affects how it compares with other robots. The caveat is printed with the figure.

Stair step and payload each exist in two variants. Only compare fields with the same label.
