Notes

  • The producer's own product page carries two conflicting technical summaries for the same robot: a Chinese banner states height 180cm, 37 total degrees of freedom, and a 9km/h maximum speed; a separate, more detailed English "Product Highlights" section further down the SAME page instead states 165cm, 41 degrees of freedom, and a maximum speed of at least 6km/h. The banner's conflicting figures are given alongside each figure.
  • A maximum joint torque of 360 N.m appears in the Chinese banner, and a separate "up to 83 N.m per kg" torque-density figure appears in the English section; these are different kinds of measurement (absolute torque vs torque per kilogram of actuator weight).
  • A manipulation accuracy of up to 0.1mm is claimed ("visual fusion control").
