Notes

  • X Square Robot's own site presents this model under its Chinese name, "Quantum No.1 Pro", branded QUANTA X1 PRO; it moves on a two-wheel differential-drive chassis, not legs.
  • The producer's own "max physical size" row gives three figures together — shoulder width 658mm, height 1690mm, chassis length 618mm — without further breakdown.
  • A whole-body "task payload capability" of 6 kg is stated without specifying whether it applies to walking or standing. A separate single-arm figure of 7 kg for 30 seconds is an arm capacity, not a whole-body figure.
  • The vertical workspace is stated as 0-1.6 m via a back-lift mechanism.
  • The onboard SDK is described as "multi-language" but only Python is explicitly named.
  • Connectivity is stated as WiFi 6 (dual-band 2.4/5GHz), Bluetooth 5.2 and 5G.
