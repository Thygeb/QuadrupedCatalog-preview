Use cases: Inspection, surveillance, transport, research in complex environments

The first quote is from the 'At A Glance' table's 'Use cases' row; the second is from the FAQ section 'What are the primary use cases for NEURA Quadruped?'. Our own interpretation: Inspection -> inspection; surveillance -> security and surveillance; transport/payload transport -> logistics; research (in complex environments) -> research and development; industrial and service applications -> industrial.
