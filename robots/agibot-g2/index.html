Notes

  • AGIBOT's own site lists G2 as part of its "Genie" series alongside G1, describing it as an "Industrial-Grade Interactive Embodied Operation Robot".
  • G2 moves on an omnidirectional wheeled chassis; the "knee" and "ankle" 2-DOF joints named in the spec sheet fold the torso through a height range of 1225-1795 mm rather than providing a walking gait.
  • Dual-arm payload is stated as 5 kg rated per arm (10 kg rated combined, 15 kg peak combined) - an arm figure, not a whole-body payload.
  • A per-joint degrees-of-freedom breakdown (head 3, arms 7 each x2, waist 3, knee/ankle 2) is given alongside a separate stated total of 25 excluding end effectors; the breakdown as given sums to 22, not 25. Both figures are the producer's own.
  • Dimensions, DOF, payload, sensor and compute figures come from AGIBOT's own G2 user manual document, a separate page from the main G2 product page.
