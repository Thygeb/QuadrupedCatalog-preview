Notes

  • This record covers Persona AI's legged humanoid platform. Persona AI's own site does not give the robot a distinct public product name; the only designation used in the company's own text is the internal generation label "Gen 1" (persona.ai/robot-welding-in-a-real-industrial-environment/, checked 25 Sep 2026). Later generations ("Gen 3") are referenced only in third-party press quoting the CEO, not on Persona AI's own site.
  • Persona AI's own website publishes no numeric technical specifications at all as of 25 Sep 2026 — height, weight, degrees of freedom, payload, speed, battery capacity/runtime, and sensor details are absent from every page checked (home, humanoid/industrial-design, why-persona/about, shipyards, construction).
  • The only documented physical demonstration is an untethered, teleoperated weld performed by the 'Gen 1' unit at ARC Specialties in Houston: the robot lowers into a squat, executes the weld, and stands back up under its own power (persona.ai/robot-welding-in-a-real-industrial-environment/), which is the evidence used here that the platform is leg-based, not wheeled.
  • A third-party aggregator (originofbots.com) publishes numeric specs attributed to this robot (height 175 cm, weight 85 kg, speed 5 km/h, and an implausible '5 years' battery life), but these are not sourced to Persona AI's own material and are deliberately not used here, per the project's rule against secondary-only numeric sources.
