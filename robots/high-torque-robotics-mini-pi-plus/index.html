Notes

  • High Torque Robotics' own pages disagree on several figures. Height and weight: the product page gives 75.6 cm and 15 kg, the store page 680 mm and 15.4 kg (Base) or 15.6 kg (Pro), the documentation about 75 cm and about 13.84 kg. Degrees of freedom: 23/27 on the product page, 22 on the store page, 27 in the documentation.
  • The store lists two versions: Base Version at $5,500 with an RK3588 CPU, and Pro Version at $7,500 with an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX and a D435i depth camera. The producer's own store lists both versions with a price and an "Add to cart" button.
  • The documentation describes Mini Pi+ as evolved from Mini Pi: the legs are reused unchanged, and the waist, arms and head are added through quick-release interfaces.
