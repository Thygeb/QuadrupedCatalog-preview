Notes

  • The producer's GR-3 series has two models with the same headline figures: GR-3 (nickname "Meow-bot"), built for interaction, and GR-3C ("Cosmo"), which adds a lidar module and a reinforced shell. The brochure lists the lidar and camera options under GR-3C.
  • The brochure lists a single-arm load capacity of 3 kg. That is an arm-specific figure, not a figure for what the whole robot can carry.
  • The brochure gives a 31-sensor tactile system (16 in the head, 15 in the torso) and a four-microphone array. It names no price; sales enquiries go through the producer's sales team.
