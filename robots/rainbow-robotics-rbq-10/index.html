Intended use, per the manufacturer

The manufacturer’s words

Research Platform

Based on dynamics-based gait control and AI gait technology, it provides walking stability and mobility outdoors, while supporting sensor and system expansion for field applications such as patrol, inspection, and observation.

PTZ cameras, thermal imaging, lighting, speakers, and other modules can be combined according to mission purpose and environment, supporting scenarios such as surveillance, inspection, and observation.

Source: rainbow-robotics.com · Retrieved 2026-08-19

'Research Platform' is a label on the product itself, alongside 'Outdoor Walking' and 'AI Gait'. L22: 'patrol' and 'surveillance' are mapped to security and surveillance; the third quote is the product page's own module description. The manufacturer's industry list for the whole company also mentions Defense & Security and Logistics, but it is not written about RBQ-10 specifically and is not used.
