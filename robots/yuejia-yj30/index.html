Notes

  • The manufacturer's own table has a typo in this model's dimensions, missing a letter from the unit, on both the Chinese and English pages. Read as millimetres, not metres.
  • The manufacturer's table merges "climb angle" and "degrees of freedom" under one heading but gives only one value, with a degree sign — so degrees of freedom is left unstated.
  • The manufacturer's own table gives two runtime figures in the same cell: 1.5-2 hours standard, and 3 hours with an optional second battery. The standard figure is used.
  • The manufacturer's marketing text hints at a laser/vision sensor system, but the specification table has no LiDAR row — LiDAR is marked not disclosed, not inferred from the marketing text.
