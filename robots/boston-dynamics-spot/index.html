Deviations and variants in the key figures (3)

Weight

CaveatThe manufacturer's own datasheet states 32.7 kg (72.1 lbs) - https://bostondynamics.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/spot-specifications.pdf. Both figures are internally consistent metric/imperial; neither can be dismissed. The difference is 1.1 kg.

Payload, walking

CaveatThe manufacturer states 'PAYLOAD MOUNTING / Max Weight' and does NOT distinguish walking from standing payload. Placement under walking payload is our interpretation, not a direct reading. The datasheet's body text states the same figure as 'up to 14 kg (30 lbs)' - 30 vs 30.9 lbs in the same document.

Runtime

CaveatThe manufacturer's footnote: "Runtime may vary depending on payloads and environmental factors.". Standby is disclosed as 180 min.
