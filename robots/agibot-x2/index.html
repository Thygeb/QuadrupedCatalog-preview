Notes

  • AGIBOT's page (https://www.agibot.com/products/X2) names this product line "AGIBOT X2 series" and presents a joint product-specifications table for two configurations, "X2" and "X2 Ultra". The page states "All the above contents and images refer to the X2 Ultra. For other models, please refer to the product specification sheet."
  • X2 Ultra adds a 3D LiDAR, RGB-D camera, front dual RGB + rear RGB cameras, a 4G/5G module, 2x RJ45 LAN ports, an Orin NX (157 TOPS) high-performance compute board, and secondary-development support, none of which base X2 has ("Not equipped" / "Not supported" in X2's own column).
  • Page states "AGIBOT X2 does not support do not support these functions" [sic, producer's own wording] regarding autonomous navigation and proactive obstacle avoidance, which the page attributes to X2 Ultra's LiDAR/RGB-D sensing.
